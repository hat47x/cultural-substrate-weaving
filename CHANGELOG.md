# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Added `10-integration`: raising a whole picture out of many fragments (carding, grouping, labelling, placement, narration) with blank detection as its primary product.
- `02-system-selection`: distinguish a framework's position layer from its interpretive layer, decided by predicate type (counting / computing / similarity). Only the position layer classifies; the interpretive layer generates.
- `01-scope-and-facts`: write the baseline out as a finished product rather than self-reporting it. Increment cannot be measured by coverage of the exploration axes.
- `00-iteration`: change the question each pass; blanks instruct the next round of gathering; try an untried stage before stopping.
- `02-system-selection`: added the removal check as a fourth post-adoption check — delete the framework's names and tables and judge finding by finding what still stands. It asks whether a finding survives, not whether the question was re-derivable without the framework, so the position layer's output is not discarded. Adopted frameworks now land in one of four states, and the signs of over-application are named.
- `01-scope-and-facts`: list what must not be broken alongside the baseline. A change the writer makes to the problem statement is not a finding of this method.
- `00-iteration`: do not file a pass's output back into the existing structure; hand off blanks that have become domain implementation questions instead of filling them.
- `10-integration`: a question from an external framework is not observed fact until returned to the target. Zero cross-check difference shows traceability, not completeness.
- `10-integration`: covering the material cuts lifted wording but not distance from it — the space it opens fills with the analyst's vocabulary, and the actor drops out, turning what someone chose into something that occurred. Measured, so the clause now carries its own cost. Also: settle whether a label or its grouping is at fault by raising the label twice; matching stumbles indict the grouping.
- `10-integration`: carding, labelling, and folding labels together are stated once as a single operation called at different grains, rather than as separate steps with separate rules. Its boundary rule runs in both directions — join to protect a meaning, split to protect the state of the evidence — and its label is raised from extracted cores with the material covered, then checked back against it. Also: keep the source separate from the path it was found by; do not group by source or speaker; do not count a repost or a derived card as an independent appeal; and name the four things a return check finds added.
- `08-governance-and-records`, `09-evaluation-and-domains`: record judgment origin, preserved items, and adoption state; do not count one change several times.
- `02-system-selection`: split the position layer into a derivable part and a conventional part. The conventional part carries the lineage, so declare which lineage, cite it, and name an alternative; a lineage declared from recall alone is a self-report rather than a checkable operation. Check the derivable part mechanically before use.
- `08-governance-and-records`: keep framework content out of the static layer. The static layer holds the procedure for retrieving, checking, and returning it — not the tables.
- `02-system-selection`: a misfit you could have written before looking at the target is not a finding about it — list the framework's structural blind spots before applying it, and subtract them afterward. And record what share of a convention's vocabulary ever matched: two conventions can agree because both missed the same way.
- `02-system-selection`: a determinate assignment is not a correct one — a mechanical predicate reproduces its errors as faithfully as its results, which is what makes them correctable. Take what filled a position back to the target. And separate the two kinds of empty position: no candidate present, versus candidates present but the relation never named.
- `02-system-selection`: counting something does not make it a counting predicate. Only mechanical membership qualifies; counting a set the analyst rules on item by item is a similarity judgment whose numeric output hides the judgment. Report the margin that decided the value, and declare whether the predicate applies to the events narrated or to the act of narrating.
- `02-system-selection`: a framework supplies the number of positions but not the rule for cutting the target into that many; the cut is part of the assignment. Settle whether the external convention is really in place by assigning twice independently, and do not build a conclusion on a position that came out split. Self-reported uncertainty does not predict which positions split.
