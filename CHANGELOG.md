# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Added `10-integration`: raising a whole picture out of many fragments (carding, grouping, labelling, placement, narration) with blank detection as its primary product.
- `02-system-selection`: distinguish a framework's position layer from its interpretive layer, decided by predicate type (counting / computing / similarity). Only the position layer classifies; the interpretive layer generates.
- `01-scope-and-facts`: write the baseline out as a finished product rather than self-reporting it. Increment cannot be measured by coverage of the exploration axes.
- `00-iteration`: change the question each pass; blanks instruct the next round of gathering; try an untried stage before stopping.
- `02-system-selection`: added the removal check as a fourth post-adoption check — delete the framework's names and tables and judge finding by finding what still stands. It asks whether a finding survives, not whether the question was re-derivable without the framework, so the position layer's output is not discarded. Adopted frameworks now land in one of four states, and the signs of over-application are named.
- `01-scope-and-facts`: list what must not be broken alongside the baseline. A change the writer makes to the problem statement is not a finding of this method.
- `00-iteration`: do not file a pass's output back into the existing structure; hand off blanks that have become domain implementation questions instead of filling them.
- `10-integration`: a question from an external framework is not observed fact until returned to the target. Zero cross-check difference shows traceability, not completeness.
- Local inspection of the accumulated edits, no new rules: the assignment rule and the external convention it introduces now precede the clauses that refer to them (they had drifted below through successive insertions); the substitution check no longer sets a contribution figure that the removal check then contradicts; the five likely additions moved into the operation that runs at every grain, from the audit that runs once at the end; the operation is listed as running at every fold, not only at carding and labelling; and the router's stop test carries the conclusion-movement criterion the detail file gained.
- `00-iteration`: tell whether a pass froze by whether the conclusion moved, not by whether the text grew. Measured: over six passes on one target, asking for a single conclusion each time and showing only the history of conclusions, both control runs reinforced early — one froze on pass 1→2, the other through 2→3 — while both skill runs replaced the central claim at every one of five transitions. Three blind judges, unaware two conditions existed, agreed 95–100% on whether a transition moved and put the two arms at 5/5 against 4/5 and 3/5. Single-pass comparison had shown nothing; so had counting findings per pass. **Corrected on re-measurement:** re-judged blind against six series including a two-clause arm, the same histories came out 5/5 and 4/5 for the skill arm against 4/5 and 3/5 twice, and the two-clause arm landed on exactly the numbers the control arm had, so those two clauses are not what produced the separation. One series moved by a point between judgings on identical text, so the gap is about one transition wide and sits inside how sensitive the instrument is to what it is compared against. The diagnostic stands; the effect size is not established.
- `ROUTER`: the minimum procedure named only one route to an increment. It now names both — take structure from a framework, or integrate the target's own material and find its blanks — with the reading order pointing at `10-integration` when material is plentiful or the structure unsettled.
- `06-output-and-collaboration`: put the falsification condition into the artifact, not only the working record.
- `10-integration`, `01-scope-and-facts`, `03-transformation`, `02-system-selection`: five clauses that loosen rather than restrain, from two handover documents. A card carries four fields and no more — source, path, stage, and who collected it when — with no field for confidence, role, importance, feeling, or type; those belong in the sentence, since a type assigned up front is what every later look goes through. A field whose value repeats may be held on the set and written back when cards of different origin meet. Of these, one item per card and a readable sentence are attested KJ practice; source, path and collector are not attested in the public sources and are carried as modern additions. An attention you cannot give a reason for goes onto a card without one; how strongly something catches you and how likely your reading is to be right are separate. Separate observation, recurrence, function, and intent — function may be named, intent is not to be read. Do not set the threshold for collecting at the threshold for publishing. And what a framework produced is kept with its provenance rather than held back.
- `00-principles-and-constraints`: the analyst's own values and default stances are structures brought in from outside the target, and get the same checks as a cultural framework; where a constraint means weakening or cutting the account, record what was lost and count that loss the way an addition is counted. Also: do not widen an entrusted scope, and do not narrow it through excessive self-restraint.
- `01-scope-and-facts`: do not infer sameness or centrality from how often something recurs; establish what each occurrence does first.
- `09-evaluation-and-domains`: do not balance an account into "problems on both sides" where an asymmetry of power, responsibility, or harm actually exists.
- `10-integration`: record the differences dropped on purpose, and add change-of-confidence to what a return check looks for.
- `10-integration`: covering the material cuts lifted wording but not distance from it — the space it opens fills with the analyst's vocabulary, and the actor drops out, turning what someone chose into something that occurred. Measured, so the clause now carries its own cost. Also: settle whether a label or its grouping is at fault by raising the label twice; matching stumbles indict the grouping.
- `10-integration`: carding, labelling, and folding labels together are stated once as a single operation called at different grains, rather than as separate steps with separate rules. Its boundary rule runs in both directions — join to protect a meaning, split to protect the state of the evidence — and its label is raised from extracted cores with the material covered, then checked back against it. Also: keep the source separate from the path it was found by; do not group by source or speaker; do not count a repost or a derived card as an independent appeal; and name the four things a return check finds added.
- `08-governance-and-records`, `09-evaluation-and-domains`: record judgment origin, preserved items, and adoption state; do not count one change several times.
- `02-system-selection`: split the position layer into a derivable part and a conventional part. The conventional part carries the lineage, so declare which lineage, cite it, and name an alternative; a lineage declared from recall alone is a self-report rather than a checkable operation. Check the derivable part mechanically before use.
- `08-governance-and-records`: keep framework content out of the static layer. The static layer holds the procedure for retrieving, checking, and returning it — not the tables.
- `02-system-selection`: a misfit you could have written before looking at the target is not a finding about it — list the framework's structural blind spots before applying it, and subtract them afterward. And record what share of a convention's vocabulary ever matched: two conventions can agree because both missed the same way.
- `02-system-selection`: a determinate assignment is not a correct one — a mechanical predicate reproduces its errors as faithfully as its results, which is what makes them correctable. Take what filled a position back to the target. And separate the two kinds of empty position: no candidate present, versus candidates present but the relation never named.
- `02-system-selection`: counting something does not make it a counting predicate. Only mechanical membership qualifies; counting a set the analyst rules on item by item is a similarity judgment whose numeric output hides the judgment. Report the margin that decided the value, and declare whether the predicate applies to the events narrated or to the act of narrating.
- `02-system-selection`: a framework supplies the number of positions but not the rule for cutting the target into that many; the cut is part of the assignment. Settle whether the external convention is really in place by assigning twice independently, and do not build a conclusion on a position that came out split. Self-reported uncertainty does not predict which positions split.
- `02-system-selection`: the mechanical check applied to the derivable part had been written as a bar on the whole position layer — "do not use a structure you cannot check" — which, read as written, disqualifies a framework that has no derivable part at all. Found in execution: running the file end to end against the five Confucian relations, the run reported that the framework's position set cannot be machine-checked and so is structurally weak as a position layer, having just used it correctly under a counting predicate. The bar now falls on a derivable part that fails its check, and where there is no derivable part, what gets checked is the external convention instead.
