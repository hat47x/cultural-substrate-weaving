# Cultural Substrate Weaving

Use cultural, philosophical, and traditional perspectives to reconsider a target's essential structure and turn discoveries into usable questions, comparisons, and compositions.

Essential structure means a set of relations, conditions, and changes that explains how the target works or differs for the current purpose. A framework can supply a higher-level perspective on a problem set. It does not guarantee a unique essence or truth about the target.

## Start from the request

Identify the purpose, target material, current understanding, and what must be preserved. When explicitly requested, perform the specified operations. When selection and judgment are delegated, choose concrete proposals within that scope and explain why.

This skill does not independently decide values, usage scope, loading depth, stopping, adoption, publication, or action. Those decisions belong to the author, or to settings, instructions, and delegations that the author gives to the generative AI outside this skill. A task category is not by itself an automatic activation or suppression condition owned by this skill.

Use polysemy as a resource for making meaning concrete in a situation. Select relations through the practitioner's work and values, and develop them into useful understanding, expression, or composition. Do not require an exhaustive list of interpretations or a scoring rubric in advance. When the reading and human interpretation reshape the question, carry that change into the next reading.

**Value being changed by the target more than having your first interpretation turn out to be right.** Emptying yourself before the target does not mean withholding hypotheses. Run framework rules boldly as provisional assumptions and let the target revise the result.

## Working outline

1. **Receive the target.** Retain the current question and material in target language. For a problem set, align the comparison units and scope.
2. **Open a perspective.** Briefly explain selection and read native distinctions, positions, relations, changes, practices, and lineage to the depth needed. Do not start with isolated similar symbols.
3. **Reconsider the target.** Use problem-set placement, relational reinterpretation, or native operations as relevant. Give reasons for qualitative readings and show alternatives that affect the choice.
4. **Return to the target.** Consult existing sources, concrete examples, exceptions, and the author's judgment. Notice the target pushing back against the framework through misfit, resistance, reversal, or excess, and revise the reading.
5. **Make the result usable.** Show in target language what became visible and which choices or compositions change. Produce comparisons, questions, design proposals, compositions, or recommendations within delegated scope. Empirical experiments are not a mandatory stage in every use.

This outline permits returns; it does not require filling every stage, framework, or operation. One capability alone is a valid use. Short prose can suffice for a small task.

## Preserve provenance

Track discovery origin, target-side support, and adoption separately. When a framework-generated question later gains support from target sources, do not rewrite its origin.

`framework_generated` denotes framework-derived candidates; `cross_field_emergent` denotes a third structure arising through contact and mutual revision; `target_supported` denotes independent target-side support; `unresolved` denotes material without a judgment yet.

**Provenance ≠ action permission**. **Correspondence != synthesis**. Removing framework vocabulary does not add target evidence. Do not promote a third structure to target-side fact without independent target-side support. Qualitative recommendations and compositions can be proposed with their reasons. See [principles](core/principles-and-constraints.md).

## Connect when needed

Delegate material-led affinity diagramming to an available `affinity-synthesis` or compatible realization. It can support material understanding before perspective analysis and reintegration after discovery. Delegate multi-round delta reopening to `iterative-inquiry-synthesis` or a compatible realization. CSW does not own those internal algorithms. Do not claim that affinity synthesis or multi-round orchestration was executed when it was not. CSW interpretation and discovery remain usable without those realizations.

Receive domain expertise, quality criteria, and value criteria from the caller's context or companion domain skills. For Japanese prose, an available external `yomiyasu` can support polishing when relevant. Settle the content first and preserve meaning and provenance during revision. It is not a required dependency.
