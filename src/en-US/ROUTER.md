# Explore and integrate target structure with cultural frameworks and KJ

This skill is a cognitive tool for using cultural frameworks and KJ to expand ways of seeing a target, questions, relations, transitions, and structural candidates, while integrating heterogeneous material.

**The skill itself is not the final authority over whether it should be used, how far it should be used, how deeply a framework should be opened, or what should be adopted, stopped, published, or moved into action.** Those decisions come from the author or from settings, instructions, and delegation given to the model outside this skill.

The skill mainly combines two capabilities.

1. **Exploration through cultural frameworks**: use positions, relations, states, transitions, cycles, directions, paths, layers, and symbols as cognitive resources for generating questions and structural candidates.
2. **Integration through KJ**: do not force heterogeneous fragments into prior categories; let semantic units, relations, contradictions, isolates, blanks, and weak unease arise from the material.

When KJ is used, begin from the stance expressed as 「己を空しくしてデータをして語らしめる」: make room for the data to speak rather than fixing your own explanation above the target. See `core/cognitive-stance.md`.

## Separate decision authority from execution state

Use scope and loading depth are not permission levels that this skill infers automatically from task type.

When useful, labels such as

- `non_activation / limited_use / exploratory_use`
- `not_loaded / probe / preview / full / enacted`

may describe the **current execution state**. Which state applies follows instructions and delegation outside the skill. Do not apply fixed rules such as “proofreading means non-activation” or “open-ended institutional design means exploratory use.”

See `core/activation.md`.

## Minimal execution flow

1. **Receive the request and delegated range**: identify not only what is being explored, but also how much choice over candidate generation, adoption, and loading depth has been delegated to the model.
2. **Establish the cognitive stance**: when using KJ, keep interpretations where the target can revise them.
3. **Materialize the source surface**: when useful, create KJ cards while retaining concrete detail, contradictions, isolates, uncertainty, and weak unease.
4. **Open cultural-framework candidates**: recall or search structural types such as state grids, cycles and calendars, paths and flows, directions, layers, and path systems. See `methods/system-selection.md`.
5. **Bring candidates into contact with the target**: perform assignment, transition, multi-framework composition, question generation, and related operations. See `methods/framework-application.md` and `methods/transformation.md`.
6. **Preserve provenance and support state**: use labels such as `target_supported / framework_generated / cross_field_emergent / unresolved` as provenance or support-state descriptions, not as adoption permission grades.
7. **Return differences and options**: show what became newly visible, what did not change, what residuals remain, and what operations could be chosen next.
8. **Connect adoption, stopping, publication, and action to external authority**: when the model makes a judgment, keep it traceable to the external instruction or delegated authority that supports that judgment.

## Important distinctions

- **Provenance ≠ adoption**: recording where something came from is different from deciding whether to use it.
- **Support state ≠ permission level**: support in target-side material is different from deciding whether to adopt something in writing, design, research, or decision-making.
- **Cognitive mode ≠ value criterion ≠ fact candidate**: do not collapse a framework's way of seeing, its value axis, and a claim about the target into one kind of statement.
- **Execution state ≠ recommendation**: `limited_use` and `preview` do not themselves mean recommended, dangerous, successful, or failed.
- **A rule inside the skill ≠ the author's intent**: being present in the canonical text is not, by itself, justification for a rule.

### Loading approach

- Read `core/cognitive-stance.md` when establishing a KJ stance toward the target.
- Read `methods/system-selection.md` when expanding cultural-framework candidates. Its state-grid, temporal, path, and related anchors can support recall and search.
- Read `methods/framework-application.md` and `methods/transformation.md` when bringing candidate frameworks into contact with the target or composing multiple frameworks.
- Read `core/iteration.md` when handling rounds, rematerialization, or delayed reopening.
- Read `domains/human-and-taiheki.md` when using Taiheki as a cognitive resource.

Do not fix the number of references, amount of reading, order, or stopping condition inside this Router. Select them according to goals, settings, and delegation outside the skill, and keep the reason traceable when it matters.
