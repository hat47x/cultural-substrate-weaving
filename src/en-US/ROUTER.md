# Open cultural frameworks as cognitive fields, then return to the target

**This skill is a tool for cognitive operations, candidate structures, provenance, and differences. It does not independently decide values, usage scope, loading depth, stopping, adoption, publication, or action. Those decisions belong to the author, or to settings, instructions, and delegations that the author gives to the generative AI outside this skill.**

This skill opens cultural frameworks as temporary cognitive fields rather than using them as answers or classifiers.

1. **Exploration through cultural frameworks**: use positions, relations, states, transitions, cycles, and symbols as cognitive resources different from ordinary analysis. **Attend not only to fit but also to the target pushing back against the framework through misfit, resistance, reversal, or excess, and ask whether that tension produces a third structure reducible to neither side.**
2. **Integration through KJ**: raise semantic units, relations, contradictions, isolates, and blanks from heterogeneous material.

Structures from target material, cultural frameworks, and their contact can be tracked separately by provenance. Labels such as `target_supported / framework_generated / cross_field_emergent / unresolved` describe provenance or state; they do not themselves authorize or prohibit adoption, speech, externalization, or stopping.

When affinity synthesis of material or multi-round delta/reopen work is needed, delegate to a dedicated compatible realization when one is available. CSW does not own those internal algorithms.

- one-round material synthesis: `affinity-synthesis` or a compatible Method realization
- multi-round delta / reopen orchestration: `iterative-inquiry-synthesis` or a compatible Method realization

Even when no compatible realization is available, CSW can still perform cultural-framework exploration, preserve attribution, and hand off target-side verification needs. Do not claim that affinity synthesis or multi-round orchestration was executed when it was not.

When this skill is used, the KJ stance expressed as 「己を空しくしてデータをして語らしめる」 can be used as a cognitive operation. It does not mean discarding knowledge or hypotheses; it is a way to keep an explanation open to revision by the target. How far this stance or any other technique is used follows the delegation outside the skill. See `core/cognitive-stance.md`.

This skill does not itself define domain-specific knowledge, quality standards, or value criteria for the artifact. Required criteria come from the author's instructions, settings outside the skill, domain skills, or source materials.

Whether a task is open or closed, simple or complex, urgent or routine — task category is not by itself an automatic activation or suppression condition owned by this skill. When the author has delegated framework selection or loading-depth decisions to the generative AI, the AI may decide within that external delegation. If the skill is **explicitly requested**, use at least a limited pass to compare against the baseline regardless of whether the task is open or closed.

## Cognitive stance

Keep your interpretation where the target can revise it. **Value being changed by the target more than having your first interpretation turn out to be right.**

This does not mean discarding knowledge or hypotheses. After sufficient contact with the material, think boldly, but do not rewrite newly generated meaning as if the source material had stated it from the beginning.

See `core/cognitive-stance.md` for details.

## Minimal execution flow

1. **Read the external delegation**: identify the judgments, usage scope, and resource constraints entrusted by the author's request and settings or instructions outside the skill.
2. **Record the usage state**: when useful, record `non_activation / limited / exploratory` and cultural-framework depth `not_loaded / probe / preview / full / enacted` as current states. The state names do not decide the next action.
3. **Choose cognitive operations needed to carry out the delegation**: use KJ, candidate recall, framework loading, transformation, provenance recording, or other operations within the entrusted scope. If numerous or heterogeneous materials need material-led structure discovery, use the connection contract in `methods/integration.md` to hand them to a compatible synthesis realization.
4. **Explore and integrate**: obtain candidate questions, relations, states, or transitions from cultural frameworks and/or integrate material with KJ. Operations may be combined iteratively. Do not thin framework-native structure into generic language too early.
5. **Keep provenance visible**: preserve the ability to distinguish target-derived, framework-derived, contact-emergent structures, and the subject who made a judgment. If a new framework contact touches prior artifacts or residuals, use the connection contract in `core/iteration.md` to hand it to a compatible iterative realization.
6. **Return decision material**: present candidates, differences, residuals, falsifiers, and provenance. Adoption, stopping, publication, and action return to the external decision authority. If judgment was delegated to the generative AI, decide within that delegation. Do not promote a third structure to target-side fact without independent target-side support.

Framework count, loading depth, full reading, and execution of framework-native operations do not by themselves mean success, failure, or stopping. They may be used as observations when external evaluation criteria require them.

## Important distinctions

- **Usage state ≠ value judgment**: `not_loaded / probe / preview / full / enacted` describe states. Whether one is preferable is an external judgment.
- **Provenance ≠ action permission**: distinguishing target, framework, and contact provenance does not by itself decide whether something may be adopted, stated, or externalized.
- **De-binding ≠ independent observation**: a statement surviving removal of framework vocabulary and the existence of separate target-side observations or sources are different pieces of information. How much either counts as evidence follows the external evidence standard.
- **Exploration ≠ attribution**: obtaining a question from a framework and judging that the target has that structure can be recorded as different events.
- **Correspondence != synthesis**: a clean mapping to a framework is not itself a third structure. Ask whether consequential differences remained visible while being recomposed into a new distinction, relation, or question.
- **KJ use ≠ framework use**: KJ and cultural frameworks are different cognitive operations; how they are combined follows the external delegation.
- **Framework output ≠ synthesis authority**: a framework-generated candidate may enter affinity synthesis as material, but it does not get to determine grouping or labels in advance.

## Loading behavior

Avoid loading everything at once. This skill does not fix reference order, reference count, framework count, or depth as its own value judgment.

- When usage scope or a framework is specified outside the skill, follow that specification when loading references.
- When the author has delegated framework selection or depth to the generative AI, use that delegation as the authority for recalling or searching candidates and loading the needed references.
- KJ, cultural frameworks, iterative analysis, Taiheki, and the other references provide capabilities and operations. The Router does not automatically activate or suppress them merely from the task category.
- Treat target-specific information as dynamic material that can be referenced separately from static method descriptions.
