# Open cultural frameworks as cognitive fields, then return to the target

**Return structures obtained from external frameworks to the target for validation. Treat only parts independently supported by target-side material as findings about the target, and preserve the provenance of questions, hypotheses, and compositional resources generated by frameworks.**

This skill opens cultural frameworks as temporary cognitive fields rather than using them as answers or classifiers.

Use positions, relations, states, transitions, cycles, symbols, boundaries, paths, and other native structures to obtain candidates that ordinary analysis may not foreground. Then return those candidates to target-side material and distinguish what is supported, unsupported, or still only a new question.

When affinity synthesis of material or multi-round delta/reopen work is needed, delegate to a dedicated compatible realization when one is available. CSW does not own those internal algorithms.

- one-round material synthesis: `affinity-synthesis` or a compatible Method realization
- multi-round delta / reopen orchestration: `iterative-inquiry-synthesis` or a compatible Method realization

Even when no compatible realization is available, CSW can still perform cultural-framework exploration, preserve attribution, and hand off target-side verification needs. Do not claim that affinity synthesis or multi-round orchestration was executed when it was not.

This skill itself does not contain domain-specific knowledge. Receive necessary domain knowledge, quality standards, and expert procedures from the calling context, available materials, or companion domain skills.

Authority over how much to use this skill or a cultural framework, loading depth, stopping, adoption, publication, and related choices is not placed in the skill's static rules. Follow requester or author instructions, higher-level settings or instructions, or delegation given to the executing AI.

## Cognitive stance

Keep your interpretation where the target can revise it.

This does not mean discarding knowledge or hypotheses. After sufficient contact with the material, think boldly, but do not rewrite newly generated meaning as if the source material had stated it from the beginning.

See `core/cognitive-stance.md` for details.

## Minimal execution flow

1. **Receive external use conditions**: identify the entrusted task, purpose, where judgment is reserved, and what discretion has been delegated to the executing AI.
2. **Establish the cognitive stance**: follow `core/cognitive-stance.md` and do not fix your explanation above the target.
3. **Set a baseline and preservation set**: inspect source material, target-specific facts and exceptions, and the domain-method-only baseline.
4. **Delegate material synthesis when needed**: if numerous or heterogeneous materials need material-led structure discovery, use the connection contract in `methods/integration.md` to hand them to a compatible synthesis realization.
5. **Open cultural frameworks**: according to external use conditions and delegated discretion, use states such as `not_loaded / probe / preview / full / enacted` as appropriate.
6. **Explore**: obtain candidate questions, relations, states, transitions, and correspondences from the framework. Do not thin framework-native structure into generic language too early.
7. **Return to the target**: keep `target_supported / framework_generated / cross_field_emergent / unresolved` distinguishable.
8. **Hand off a round delta when needed**: if a new framework contact touches prior artifacts or residuals, use the connection contract in `core/iteration.md` to hand it to a compatible iterative realization.
9. **Reflect into real work**: carry results into artifacts, decision material, research directions, residuals, and reopening conditions. Boundaries and adoption follow external use conditions.

Do not encode framework count, full reading, or the amount of framework-native enactment as a success measure in itself.

## Important distinctions

- **State recording != decision authority**: distinguish preserving provenance, epistemic state, and loading depth from deciding adoption, stopping, or publication based on those distinctions.
- **Preservation != current attention**: keep canonical material rich; foreground only what current work needs.
- **Exploration != attribution**: getting a question from a framework is not the same as claiming that the target has that structure.
- **De-binding != evidence**: a statement surviving removal of framework vocabulary does not create independent target-side support.
- **Use state != success judgment**: loading depth or rejection is an exploration state, not success or failure by itself.
- **Framework output != synthesis authority**: a framework-generated candidate may enter affinity synthesis as material, but it does not get to determine grouping or labels in advance.
- **Synthesis result != framework verification**: sharing a group, appearing nearby, or acquiring a relation or resonance does not independently validate the framework.

## Loading behavior

Do not assume that everything must be loaded at once.

- Read `core/cognitive-stance.md` when the cognitive stance needs to be re-established.
- Use `core/activation.md` when handling framework use depth.
- Read the relevant method when framework selection or a native operation is needed.
- For one-round material synthesis, delegate through the connection contract to a compatible realization.
- For multi-round reopening, delegate through the connection contract to a compatible iterative realization.

Keep target-specific information in the dynamic material layer rather than absorbing it into the static Skill.
