# Rasa–bhāva aesthetic theory

In the tradition rooted in the Nāṭyaśāstra, bhāvas embodied in performance are not simply identical to rasa aesthetically tasted by the spectator. Aesthetic experience arises through performance, context, expression, and reception. Older formulations center eight principal rasas; śānta is widely associated with later expansion to nine.

CSW uses rasa as a culturally specific aesthetic framework, not universal emotion psychology. Strong operations include production/reception separation, ingredient passes, provisional dominant-flavor probes, affect transitions, and cultural-specificity checks.

Keep the eight/nine distinction visible, do not identify everyday emotion with aesthetic rasa, and do not diagnose real people's emotions. Actual audience reception requires reception evidence.
