# Wuxing / Five Phases

Wuxing relates wood, fire, earth, metal, and water as phases or processes. CSW foregrounds generation (sheng) and overcoming/restraint (ke). A common generation sequence is wood → fire → earth → metal → water → wood; a common overcoming sequence is wood → earth → water → fire → metal → wood. Domain-specific correspondences are separate layers.

## Strong operations

- dual-cycle support/restraint pass;
- role reversal across generating, receiving, restraining, and being restrained;
- dynamic balance through circulation rather than equal counts;
- missing-link probe for broken relations;
- phase-shift probe rather than fixed traits.

Do not flatten Wuxing into five adjectives or convert traditional medical correspondences into modern diagnosis. Attribution requires independent target-side assignment and evidence.
