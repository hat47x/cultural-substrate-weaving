# Connection to material synthesis

Read this when numerous or heterogeneous materials need structure to emerge without imposing a prior classification axis.

This document does not implement affinity synthesis itself. CSW owns cultural-framework exploration and attribution; internal material-synthesis procedures are delegated to a dedicated Method / compatible realization.

## Responsibility boundary

When target material needs affinity synthesis, use `affinity-synthesis` or another compatible realization satisfying the same Method Definition when available.

Delegate these concerns to the material-synthesis layer:

- meaning-bearing card boundary
- epistemic seam
- grouping / higher-order grouping
- label / group advocacy statement
- singleton / conflict / residual
- relational structure
- distinction among membership / relation / secondary resonance / layout
- source return check
- inherited / emergent / residual transformation audit
- diagram <-> narrative check
- provenance / derivation lineage and double-count prevention

CSW does not independently re-implement these internal algorithms.

## What CSW hands into synthesis

Questions, hypotheses, contrasts, correspondence candidates, and compositional candidates generated by a cultural framework may enter affinity synthesis as material while preserving their origin.

When needed, keep at least these distinguishable:

- `framework_generated`
- `cross_field_emergent`
- `unresolved`
- `target_supported` when independently supported by target-side material

This attribution information is not the first axis of grouping geometry. It exists so later work can return to and distinguish target, framework, and contact-derived material.

A framework-generated candidate receives no authority bonus, independent support, or factual status merely because a framework produced it.

Internal framework consistency, sharing a group in affinity synthesis, diagram proximity, secondary resonance, or an explicit relation is not independent target-side corroboration by itself.

## Receiving synthesis output

When affinity synthesis returns a result, CSW keeps at least these distinct:

1. meaning supported by target-side material;
2. questions or correspondence candidates originating from a framework;
3. meaning newly produced by contact between them; and
4. residuals whose attribution, confidence, or usefulness remains unsettled.

Even when synthesis produces natural language and removes framework vocabulary, do not backdate framework-derived meaning as something the target material had stated from the beginning.

When useful, preserve meaning separately from origin and verification state.

```text
meaning: <natural-language synthesis>
origin: framework_generated | cross_field_emergent | target-side material lineage
verification: target_supported | unresolved | other explicit state
```

This is not a fixed schema.

## Synthesis may correct a framework reading

If affinity synthesis or later target material does not support a framework-derived correspondence candidate, do not force the target to fit the framework.

Allow the correspondence to be:

- weakened;
- split;
- withdrawn;
- returned to `unresolved`; or
- reopened through another framework contact.

CSW is not trying to make the framework fit the target completely.

## When no compatible realization is available

If `affinity-synthesis` or another compatible realization is unavailable, CSW can still perform:

- framework exploration;
- framework-generated questions / hypotheses / correspondence candidates;
- attribution / provenance preservation;
- target-side verification questions; and
- preparation of handoff material.

Do not describe KJ / affinity synthesis as executed when it was not actually run.

If a simple summary or fixed classification is used instead, do not relabel it as affinity synthesis.

## Minimal handoff

When handing work to material synthesis, include only what the current task needs, such as:

- synthesis subject / current purpose;
- target-side material refs;
- framework-generated / cross-field candidates and their origin;
- known residual / conflict / unresolved items; and
- provenance / derivation needed for audit.

Do not duplicate the complete history every time.

## CSW canonical responsibilities

Higher-level principles remain in `core/principles-and-constraints.md`.

In particular, CSW retains:

- dual fidelity;
- attribution principles;
- target specificity;
- separation of preservation from current attention;
- separation of possibility from adoption; and
- separation of cognition, value, and fact.

Do not copy the internal material-synthesis Method back into CSW.
