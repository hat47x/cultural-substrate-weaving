# Governance

Japanese (`ja-JP`) is the semantic canonical locale. Maintainers may accept improvements originating in any locale, but method-level changes are first reconciled in the Japanese canonical source and then propagated.

Release status by locale is recorded in `i18n/translation-manifest.json`. A translation may be distributed with a review warning, but it must not be described as authoritatively equivalent without independent review.

Platform adapters are generated from source and templates. Generated artifacts do not establish new method rules.
