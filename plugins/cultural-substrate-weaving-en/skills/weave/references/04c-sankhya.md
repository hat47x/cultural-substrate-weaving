# Sāṅkhya

Classical Sāṅkhya is commonly organized as twenty-five tattvas and distinguishes puruṣa, the witnessing person, from prakṛti, the productive side of nature. Accounts distinguish unmanifest and manifest nature and a sequence including intellect, ego, capacities, subtle elements, and gross elements. Prakṛti is described through sattva, rajas, and tamas.

CSW does not adopt this metaphysics as fact. It uses layer backtracking, production trees, observer/process separation, provisional guṇa-mixture probes, and inversion of assumed causes.

Do not collapse the classical 25-tattva system into later cosmologies or automatically merge it with chakra, Ayurveda, or Vedānta systems. Do not use guṇas as fixed personality diagnosis.
