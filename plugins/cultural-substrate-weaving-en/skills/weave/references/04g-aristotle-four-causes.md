# Aristotle's four causes

Aristotle distinguishes material, formal, efficient, and final causes as different ways of answering why; more than one may be relevant to one phenomenon. CSW uses this to pluralize explanation, not as a direct replacement for modern scientific causation.

Strong operations include why-splitting, explanation-gap detection, causal-category audit, multi-cause composition, and artifact/design probes.

Do not translate every aitia into modern efficient causation or attribute final causes without domain-specific warrant. Generate explanation candidates here; determine actual causes with domain evidence.
