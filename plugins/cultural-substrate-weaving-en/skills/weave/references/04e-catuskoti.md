# Catuṣkoṭi / tetralemma

The catuṣkoṭi opens four corners around P: P, not-P, both P and not-P, and neither P nor not-P. Uses vary by Buddhist context and lineage; Madhyamaka strongly associates it with dismantling fixed views.

CSW does not freeze this into a modern four-valued truth table. Strong operations are binary-break, both/neither probes, predicate audit, and de-reification.

Do not assume the four corners are always four simultaneously true propositions. Use the framework to disrupt question framing, not as evidence that a target is both or neither.
