# Make discovery concrete

Use framework contact to generate questions, distinctions, relations, and compositions the current understanding does not readily suggest, then make them usable in target language.

Run native rules boldly under the assumption of provisionally adopting the framework's view. Do not require proof of a conclusion during generation. Keep the candidates' origin distinct from what can be said about the target.

## Use native operations

Preserve the framework's relevant whole and select operations that serve the current question. The following are entry points, not a checklist to complete. Identify analyst-added operations where the framework does not supply them.

| Resource | Operation | Return to the target |
|---|---|---|
| Distinctions and positions | Read the same target from another position | Distinctions, comparison axes, alternative explanations |
| Vacant positions | Turn a vacancy in a closed set into an unasked question | Questions and possible further sources |
| Opposition and complementarity | Read from the opposing or complementary view | Hidden conditions and competing readings |
| Relational networks | Follow adjacency, generation, restraint, or paths | Relational candidates, intermediate positions, connection questions |
| Transitions and cycles | Provisionally vary sequence, return, phase, or reversibility | Conditions of change, alternative paths, compositional sequence |
| Boundaries and scale | Shift inside/outside, center/periphery, layer, or time scale | Revised comparison units and boundary questions |
| Practice | Adapt state-specific attention or action to the target situation | Attention, questions, and concrete proposals |

The first neat correspondence need not close exploration. When moving to another operation, explain what it should reveal. Operation count, framework count, and reading volume are not outcomes.

## Deepen essential structure

When deepening essential value is requested, provisionally run the framework's distinctions, relations, and changes together before translating an operation into generic questions or feature names. Read what counts as one unit in the target, what makes it possible, and what changes when its relations or conditions change. Develop a promising perspective and retain concrete target details that resist it.

Develop the consequences of changing one relation for other roles, boundaries, and enabling conditions. Distinctions such as unchanged source wording with a changed role, or identical states with different meanings because of their paths, can yield structural candidates absent from the previous explanation. Separate actual target changes from analyst assumptions. Identify consequences added by the analyst rather than specified by the framework.

When using another framework, select a perspective with low conceptual coupling and examine what can vary separately and which dependencies remain. Apply the second operation to distinctions or compositions produced by the first, then read which relations change at their intersection. Follow `02b-perspective-analysis.md` for orthogonality judgment. Two juxtaposed questions do not establish a third structure.

Derive conditions under which value becomes possible and concrete ways to satisfy those conditions. Explain what can newly be distinguished, understood, or composed alongside what is preserved. Honor entrusted preservation requirements; do not treat current representations or implementation conveniences as the upper bound on value when considering provisional compositions. Actual changes follow external delegation.

A conventional method's ability to express the same result differs from framework contact directing attention toward that question. Separate value in generating an idea from value in keeping a framework permanently in the final artifact. Contact without discernible value need not be adopted. Judge depth through what changed from the previous understanding and how that difference serves the purpose, rather than framework count, idea count, or unusualness.

This guidance applies when deep discovery is sought; it does not impose identical stages or forms on every request. Do not supply absent structure to force a framework that lacks distinctions, relations, or changes into the pattern.

## Receive differences from contact

Attend to agreement as well as misfit, resistance, reversal, excess, and mutual revision. Do not weaken target material to retain a candidate. Split, revise, or leave the correspondence undecided.

Use `02b-perspective-analysis.md` for re-partitioning through another perspective. New distinctions or relations can be recorded as third structures under `03-transformation.md`. When none emerges, retain misfit and unresolved connections as they are.

## Return to target language

Restate candidates as questions, comparisons, or compositions usable without knowing the framework name or symbols. Candidates losing meaning without that vocabulary may remain culture-specific compositional resources with their origin retained.

For example, a vacant complementary position might suggest asking whose perspective is not yet recorded. Vacancy alone does not establish a missing record as fact.

Target return can use existing sources, concrete examples, exceptions, domain judgment, and discussion with the caller. Do not uniformly demand new empirical experiments or numerical evaluation. Unsupported fact candidates remain hypotheses; if used in a composition, state that purpose.

## Realize in the deliverable

| Exit | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Question or comparison | What comparison distinguishes the current reading from another |
| Interpretation or explanation | Relations or conditions newly visible, and remaining limits |
| Design or composition | Concrete rearrangement, preservation requirements, and selection rationale |
| Recommendation or judgment | A selected option under delegated purpose and criteria, its basis, and remaining issues |
| Research, observation, or trial | Where and how to obtain further material when needed |

Research and trials are one exit among several. If the request calls for design or composition and existing sources plus qualitative judgment permit choosing, proceed to a concrete proposal. Receive domain-specific action judgments and quality criteria from the caller.

## Carry useful provenance

Where adoption depends on it, retain origin, assumptions, target support or counterexamples, and the judging subject. Short prose is sufficient; not every candidate needs the same form.

```text
Candidate and its use in target language:
Framework, part, operation, or contact origin:
Working assumption:
What target material supports and what remains undecided:
Adoption or recommendation rationale and judging subject:
```

Use `10-integration.md` for affinity-diagramming handoff and `00-iteration.md` when later material calls for reopening. Synthesis, adoption, or removal of assumption vocabulary alone does not add target support.

Illumination means revisiting origin, assumption, whole, target, and concrete use. It is not an acceptance gate or mandatory form. Make target facts, framework-derived ideas, and chosen proposals distinguishable to the reader.
