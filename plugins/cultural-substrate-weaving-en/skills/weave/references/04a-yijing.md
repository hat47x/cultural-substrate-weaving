# Yijing / trigrams and hexagrams

The Yijing / Zhouyi is a composite textual and commentarial tradition. CSW first preserves the reproducible structure: two line types, eight three-line trigrams, sixty-four six-line hexagrams, and positions read bottom to top. Names, images, sequences, and interpretive systems are additional historical layers.

## Strong operations

- binary differentiation across several positions;
- nesting through lower and upper trigrams;
- position sensitivity;
- mutation probe by flipping one line as a CSW sensitivity test, not silently as a lineage-specific divinatory rule;
- whole-field contrast to generate change questions rather than answers.

Do not reduce Yijing to generic yin–yang balance or assume later operations are universally canonical. Attribution to a specific hexagram requires an assignment procedure independent of resemblance.
