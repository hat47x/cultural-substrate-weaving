# Dependent origination

CSW uses the conditional pattern: when this is present, that comes to be; with the arising of this, that arises; when this ceases, that ceases. The twelve-link series is well known, but early materials also contain shorter and differing formulations, so one twelve-step list is not treated as a universal causal machine.

## Strong operations

- condition pass;
- arising/cessation pair;
- intervention-point probe;
- chain compression/expansion;
- hidden-dependency probe.

Do not reduce the framework to everything-is-connected or universalize the twelve links as a modern causal model. Actual causal claims require target-side evidence.
