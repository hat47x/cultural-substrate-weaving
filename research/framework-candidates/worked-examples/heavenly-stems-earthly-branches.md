# Heavenly Stems / Earthly Branches target-return example

Status: hypothetical / profile-readiness support

A system has two schedules that advance together once per processing step:

- schedule A has 10 positions;
- schedule B has 12 positions.

A report assumes there are 120 possible joint states because there are 10 × 12 possible mathematical pairs.

A stems/branches-inspired pass asks whether the cycles are independently selectable or synchronously advanced.

If both start from fixed origins and advance by one position at each step, the complete pair repeats after 60 steps. Only the synchronized subset of pairs is reachable.

Target-return questions become:

- Which pair states actually occur in the logs?
- Which apparent combinations are impossible under the coupling rule?
- When A returns to its first coordinate after 10 steps, what coordinate is B on?
- Would changing one schedule independently invalidate the 60-state model?

The useful output is constrained coupled-cycle reasoning, not a cultural correspondence assigned to the schedules.
