# Scholastic disputed-question negative example

Status: hypothetical / research-only

## Target

A product team has not yet chosen a problem framing. They are exploring several unrelated possibilities for a new user-facing feature and want to increase the variety of questions before narrowing scope.

## Why the framework should not be forced

There is no stable thesis or decision question yet.

Creating a "question", inventing objections to one provisional idea, and demanding a determination would prematurely turn divergent exploration into adversarial review. The result would likely privilege whichever tentative idea happened to be phrased first.

The correct CSW result is non-activation.

Useful next work belongs to material-led exploration or another framework that opens alternative configurations. If a concrete proposal later emerges, disputed-question structure may become useful for review.

## Stop condition

Stop or weaken the framework contact when:

- objections have to be invented because no target-side proposal exists;
- the question changes every time an objection is raised;
- the task is still seeking alternatives rather than reviewing a determination;
- the "reply" would require evidence that has not yet been collected.

Non-activation is preferable to manufacturing a debate.
