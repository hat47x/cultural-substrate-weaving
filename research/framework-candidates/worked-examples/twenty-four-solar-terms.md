# Twenty-Four Solar Terms target-return example

Status: hypothetical / profile-readiness support

A facilities team reviews annual cooling-system load only by calendar quarter.

Several years of telemetry show that load changes sharply near recurring points inside Q2 and Q3, but the quarter-level report averages those transitions away.

A Twenty-Four-Solar-Terms-inspired pass does not rename the transitions with Chinese phenological meanings.

It asks:

- Would finer recurring annual phase markers expose stable transition windows?
- Does the load change before, at, or after the candidate phase boundary?
- Is the change aligned with local temperature/humidity records or merely with calendar scheduling?
- Does the same phase behave differently at another site?

The useful output is a finer annual phase hypothesis returned to local telemetry, not a claim that the building follows traditional solar-term weather meanings.
