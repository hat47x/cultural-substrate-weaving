# Greco-Roman method-of-loci negative example

Status: hypothetical / research-only

## Target

A team has collected forty open-ended interview fragments about why users abandon a workflow. The fragments have no target-side chronological order, and the current task is to discover affinities, tensions, and unexpected groupings.

## Why the framework should not be forced

Assigning every fragment to L1, L2, L3 ... would manufacture an order that the material does not possess.

The fixed traversal would also encourage reviewers to treat neighboring positions as related, even though the spatial adjacency came from the mnemonic scaffold rather than from the interviews.

This is a material-led affinity task, not an ordered-reconstruction task.

The correct CSW result is non-activation.

## Stop condition

Stop or weaken the framework contact when:

- the target has no defensible sequence or stable checkpoint structure;
- positions must be invented before a target-side ordering question exists;
- spatial adjacency starts being read as semantic or causal relation;
- the scaffold is replacing, rather than reopening, the source material;
- a plain list or checklist already exposes the same omission with less overhead.

Non-activation is preferable to turning an unordered field into an artificial path.
