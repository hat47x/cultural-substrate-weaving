# Hadith isnād / matn target-return worked example

Status: hypothetical / research support for runtime adoption

A policy sentence appears in meeting notes, a chat message, and a later summary. At first glance this looks like three independent confirmations.

A transmission/content pass separates the wording from the claimed paths. The chat message and later summary both turn out to quote the same meeting statement. The three artifacts therefore form multiple documents but not three independent origins. A wording change in the summary is then compared separately from the transmission paths.

The target-facing result is a provenance graph plus content variants. No religious authenticity category is imported. The useful distinction is that independence of documents, independence of transmission paths, and truth of the proposition are three different questions.
