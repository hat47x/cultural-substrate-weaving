# Rites of passage target-return example

Status: hypothetical / profile-readiness support

An organization migrates users from an old permission model to a new one.

The migration is not instantaneous.

- old roles are first marked for retirement;
- during migration, some users temporarily retain old permissions while receiving new ones;
- incorporation is complete only after the new role is accepted, dependent workflows are updated, and old permissions are revoked.

A van-Gennep-inspired pass does not call the users ritual participants. It asks whether the transition has distinct old-state, in-between, and incorporated-state conditions.

The useful target questions are:

- What was actually separated from the old state?
- Which old permissions remain active during the intermediate state?
- What concrete event counts as incorporation into the new model?
- Can the migration stall after separation but before incorporation?
- Are mixed permissions being misclassified as a completed migration?

After de-binding, the result is a transition-state audit rather than a ritual analogy.
