# Theosophical sefirot target-return example

Status: hypothetical / profile-readiness support

A company states that “strategy becomes customer behavior through the product.”

That sentence hides several qualitatively different mediating functions. A theosophical-sefirot-inspired pass does not assign historical sefirot to the organization. It asks whether the direct link is concealing differentiated mediation.

Target-owned mediators might include:

- policy interpretation;
- product design;
- authorization;
- operational execution;
- measurement and feedback.

The useful questions are not “which sefirah is product design?” They are:

- Which mediator changes the meaning of the strategy rather than merely passing it along?
- Which mediators operate in parallel?
- Where do two functions constrain or balance one another?
- Which direct strategy→behavior claim disappears when the missing mediators are made explicit?

The framework is de-bound before attribution. Only the differentiated-mediation questions remain.
