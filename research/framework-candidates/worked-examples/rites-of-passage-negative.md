# Rites of passage negative example

Status: negative example / profile-readiness support

A feature flag changes atomically from false to true and no intermediate permissions, roles, acknowledgements, or transition behavior exist.

Imposing separation, liminality, and incorporation would manufacture stages not present in the target.

The correct CSW result is to keep the atomic state change and reject the rites-of-passage pass. A before/after distinction alone does not establish a meaningful liminal state.
