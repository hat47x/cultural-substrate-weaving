# Sefer Yetzirah dimensions source packet

Status: research-only / lineage-specific

## Scope

This packet isolates the `sefirot` language of Sefer Yetzirah from later theosophical Kabbalah. It does not treat the later Tree of Life as the unchanged meaning of the earlier text.

## Structural candidate

Potentially useful operations include:

- finite-count / open-depth tension;
- paired directions or extents;
- spatial, temporal, or evaluative axis differentiation;
- axis-based reframing rather than a fixed hierarchy.
