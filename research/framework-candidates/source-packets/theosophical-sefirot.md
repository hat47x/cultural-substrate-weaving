# Theosophical sefirot source packet

Status: research-only / religious-lineage-sensitive

## Scope

This packet isolates medieval and later theosophical Kabbalistic uses of the ten sefirot from Sefer Yetzirah's earlier usage.

## Structural candidate

Potential operations include:

- layered mediation;
- emanatory relation;
- differentiated attributes in one relational field;
- tension and balance among functions;
- vertical or relational ordering without reducing the system to a generic hierarchy.

## Source basis

## Boundary

Do not treat all medieval, Zoharic, Lurianic, Hasidic, or Western-esoteric Tree of Life diagrams as one invariant structure. Religious theology and divine attributes are not target-side facts.

Profile-ready status requires choosing a bounded historical/lineage layer and showing an operation that survives de-binding without becoming a generic organization chart.
