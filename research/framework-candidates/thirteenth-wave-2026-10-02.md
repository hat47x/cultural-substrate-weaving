# Framework corpus thirteenth wave — 2026-10-02

Status: new profile-ready candidate / no runtime adoption

## Decision

A new candidate, `inka-khipu-record-structure`, enters the corpus directly at profile-ready because its scope can be bounded to well-documented record structure without claiming general decipherment.

## Cognitive contribution

The candidate opens a previously thin operation family:

- attachment hierarchy preservation;
- position as a separate information channel;
- multi-channel record inspection;
- group/summary relation probing;
- flattening-loss audit;
- preservation of structured but undeciphered semantics.

This differs from generic trees, provenance chains, and combinatorial generation.

## Epistemic boundary

The profile deliberately separates securely understood numerical conventions in some statistical khipus from disputed or undeciphered non-numerical semantics.

Unknown structure remains unknown.

CSW must not:

- decode narrative meanings from color or knot patterns;
- treat every khipu as numerical;
- equate khipu with a spreadsheet/database/writing system as a settled historical fact;
- carry Inka archaeological structure into post-Inka or living Andean traditions without separate sourcing.

## Product-value effect

CSW gains a framework for **representation loss**: recognizing when information is carried by attachment, position, layout, material, or other channels that disappear under text-only flattening.

This extends the encounter function beyond interpretation of ideas into the structure of records and externalized memory.

No efficacy benchmark or runtime adoption is introduced.
