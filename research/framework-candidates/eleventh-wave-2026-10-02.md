# Framework corpus eleventh wave — 2026-10-02

Status: candidate-quality expansion / no runtime adoption

## Decision

The former umbrella `sefirot` research path is now operationally split into two profile-ready candidates.

### Sefer Yetzirah dimensions

The candidate is bounded by textual-version uncertainty and uses only axis/extents operations:

- dimension pairing;
- opposed extents;
- axis reframing;
- finite frame with open internal depth.

It does not import later Tree of Life attributes or treat one recension as canonical for the whole tradition.

### Theosophical sefirot

The candidate is bounded to medieval/classical theosophical research and uses:

- differentiated mediation;
- source→mediator→manifestation relations;
- attribute tension;
- relational ordering;
- configuration-variant probes.

It does not adopt one Tree of Life diagram, later lineage practice, or divine ontology.

## Product-value effect

The split increases candidate quality without inflating runtime. CSW can now investigate two structurally different cognitive jobs that were previously hidden behind the shared word `sefirot`.

This is more valuable than adding one broad “Tree of Life” framework, because the latter would create false continuity between historically different structures.

## Remaining decision

Neither candidate is adopted yet.

- Sefer Yetzirah dimensions must still show that its axis operation adds enough beyond generic dimensional analysis.
- Theosophical sefirot must still show that differentiated mediation remains distinctive after religious symbolism is removed.

Broad efficacy scoring remains unnecessary at this stage.
