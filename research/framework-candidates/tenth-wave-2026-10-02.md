# Framework corpus tenth wave — 2026-10-02

Status: bounded runtime adoption

## Decision

Ramon Llull's late Ternary Ars moves from sourced-candidate to adopted.

The adopted runtime core is deliberately narrower than “the Lullian Art” as a whole. It is restricted to the late Ternary structure represented by the `Ars generalis ultima` and `Ars brevis`, where binary and ternary combinations are generated through figures, letters, definitions, rules, and questions.

The cognitive contribution is:

- systematic combinatorial enumeration;
- crossing of distinct dimensions;
- rotation away from favored combinations;
- unseen-combination probes;
- expansion of question space.

CSW adds target-constraint pruning after generation. That pruning is not attributed to Llull.

## Product-value effect

This directly strengthens the encounter function. The adopted corpus can now do more than offer a different interpretation of an already-selected structure. It can systematically expose combinations that the current target description never placed together.

That makes the framework portfolio less dependent on spontaneous association while preserving the non-ranking boundary introduced by cognitive-need recall.

## Non-license

The runtime dossier does not import Llull's theological Dignities, missionary purpose, or claim that combinatorial procedure reveals truth. It does not collapse the Quaternary and Ternary phases.

A generated combination remains `framework_generated` until it survives target-side constraints and produces a useful question, observation, or test.
