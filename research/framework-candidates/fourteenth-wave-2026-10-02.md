# Framework corpus fourteenth wave — 2026-10-02

Status: new profile-ready candidate / no runtime adoption

## Decision

Add `vedic-recitation-pathas` as a profile-ready research candidate.

The purpose is not to add another religious framework by name.

It fills the coverage-map gap around **oral / performative memory and sequence-preservation operations**.

## Cognitive contribution

The candidate supplies a structure that was previously thin in the corpus:

- continuous vs segmented representation;
- overlapping adjacency inspection;
- alternate-view sequence cross-check;
- deliberately redundant local representation;
- order and boundary fidelity audit.

The reusable operation is not sacred recitation itself.

It is the idea that the same ordered material can be exposed through more than one explicit sequence view before a transformation is accepted as faithful.

## Source and lineage boundary

The profile separates:

- UNESCO's living-tradition record;
- a regional Nambudiri/Kerala scholarly overview;
- Staal's specialist Nambudiri study;
- historical research on kramapāṭha.

The candidate does not claim one invariant Vedic recitation system across all recensions or regions.

It also does not:

- certify pronunciation or ritual correctness;
- simulate religious authority;
- turn faithful recitation into semantic truth;
- describe the tradition as a modern error-correcting code as if that were its historical self-description.

## Near-neighbor separation

### vs Inka khipu

Khipu preserves multiple structural channels in a record artifact.

Vedic recitation pathas provide multiple sequence views of transmitted material.

### vs Hadith isnād / matn

Hadith keeps content separate from attribution/transmission path.

Vedic recitation pathas focus here on sequence form and local order, not chain credibility.

### vs checksum

A checksum/signature is stronger when the requirement is bit-exact transport integrity.

The Vedic-inspired abstraction is retained only where human-readable boundaries, adjacency, or transformations themselves need inspection.

## Product-value effect

CSW gains a candidate for a previously thin operation family:

**same-material / alternate-view fidelity checking**.

This is valuable because it is neither another taxonomy nor another metaphorical perspective.

It contributes a concrete operation for preserving and inspecting ordered information during transformation.

No runtime adoption or efficacy claim is introduced.
