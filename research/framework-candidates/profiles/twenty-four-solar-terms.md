# Twenty-Four Solar Terms candidate profile

Status: sourced-candidate / research-only

## Source basis

UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, The Twenty-Four Solar Terms

https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/the-twenty-four-solar-terms-knowledge-in-china-of-time-and-practices-developed-through-observation-of-the-suns-annual-motion-00647

UNESCO describes an annual solar motion divided into 24 named segments, used historically and in living practice as recurring temporal markers.

### Independent scholarly context

Scientific Reports, “Analysis of geographical origin of solar terms based on the STTMD method” (2024)

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-73740-x

The study independently examines the geographic and climatic origin of the solar-term system and emphasizes the middle/lower Yellow River context and later regional adaptation. CSW uses this source mainly to strengthen the boundary against treating the 24 terms as a universal climate model.

## Structural value for CSW

- finer segmentation than four seasons;
- explicit phase boundaries in an annual cycle;
- recurring position plus practice timing;
- connection between observed natural change and cultural temporal organization.

## Candidate operations

annual-phase, fine-grained-cycle-segmentation, phase-boundary, seasonal-transition, practice-timing.

## Boundaries

Do not turn traditional terms into a universal climate model, agricultural prescription, or causal explanation for unrelated targets. Regional/community uses remain contextual.

## Target-return questions

- Is a coarse “season” hiding a meaningful transition boundary?
- Which change appears before, at, or after a recurring phase marker?
- Does a practice depend on actual target conditions or only on inherited timing?

## Adoption gap

Needs a lineage/regional note and a target-return example that uses phase segmentation without pretending the target has Chinese seasonal semantics.
