# Aristotle's four causes framework profile

Status: adopted / runtime-corpus

## Identity

- names: アリストテレス四原因 / Aristotle's four causes
- family: ancient Greek Aristotelian explanatory theory
- intended CSW use: split overloaded why-questions into distinct explanatory modes
- runtime dossier: src/ja-JP/frameworks/aristotle-four-causes.md

## Source basis

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Aristotle on Causality

https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/aristotle-causality/

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Aristotle's Metaphysics

https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/aristotle-metaphysics/

The sources distinguish material, formal, efficient, and final causes and emphasize that multiple kinds can enter one explanation.

## Structural core

- material: what something is from / made of;
- formal: what-it-is / organization or form;
- efficient: source of change or rest;
- final: end or that-for-the-sake-of-which.

The Greek aitia has a broader explanatory range than the narrow modern use of “cause.”

## Lineage / variant boundary

CSW uses Aristotle's own fourfold distinction, not every later Aristotelian or medieval development. Formal and final causes may interact or coincide in Aristotelian explanations; the runtime does not force four independent boxes.

## Exploratory outputs

- four-way why-question;
- missing explanatory mode;
- category-confusion warning;
- complementary explanation set.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- treating final cause as empirical intention where no intention exists;
- translating all aitia into modern efficient causation;
- assuming all four categories must be equally present;
- using the framework instead of domain evidence.

## De-binding

Remove Aristotle labels after they have split the question. Return to target language such as material constraint, organization, triggering mechanism, or explicit purpose, and verify each independently.

## Target-return questions

- Are material constraints being confused with mechanism?
- Is structure doing explanatory work that has not been named?
- What actually initiated the change?
- Is a purpose attributed, and whose documented purpose is it?

## What this framework does not license

It does not prove teleology, intention, or causation in a target. It only broadens the kinds of explanation to investigate.
