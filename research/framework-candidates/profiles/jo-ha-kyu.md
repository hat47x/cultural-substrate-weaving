# Jo-Ha-Kyū candidate profile

Status: sourced-candidate / research-only

## Source basis

J-STAGE, Characteristics of actions for sliding walk technique in Japanese traditional performing art

https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/hcs/2014/24/2014_204/_article/-char/en

The study analyzes Jo-Ha-Kyū in Kyogen walking and reports non-monotonic tempo shaped by acceleration and deceleration rather than three equal blocks.

## Structural value for CSW

Jo-Ha-Kyū can supply temporal articulation: an opening condition, a breaking/developing acceleration, and a rapid or resolving phase. Its value is pacing and transition shape, not a three-item taxonomy.

## Candidate operations

pacing-segmentation, tempo-transition, acceleration-profile, closure-shape, nested-temporal-structure.

## Boundaries

Do not infer one universal numerical timing law from one performance study. Broader use across Noh, music, tea, or other arts needs source-specific work.

## Target-return questions

- Where does the process actually accelerate?
- Is the closing phase merely shorter, or structurally different?
- Does equal partitioning erase the temporal function of the phases?

## Adoption gap

Needs broader historical/performance sources before runtime adoption and a non-artistic target-return example to test de-binding.
