# Dependent origination runtime requalification profile

Status: profile-ready / research-only / Buddhist-tradition-sensitive / no-runtime

## Identity

- names: dependent origination / pratītyasamutpāda / paṭiccasamuppāda / 縁起
- current scope: the general conditional-arising / conditional-cessation formula and the early Buddhist dependent-origination tradition, without treating one twelve-link formulation as the only historical form
- current runtime use: move upstream from a symptom to its conditions, pair arising with cessation, and ask which condition is an actionable intervention point
- not intended use: converting Buddhist soteriology or ontology into a generic causal model, or treating a framework-shaped chain as target-side evidence

## Source basis

### Primary translation — SN 12.41

Bhikkhu Sujato, SuttaCentral, SN 12.41.

https://suttacentral.net/sn12.41/en/sujato

The text states the characteristic conditional formula in both arising and cessation directions and then gives a dependent-origination sequence. CSW uses the paired conditional structure as historical source material; the religious and soteriological claims are not transferred into target domains.

### Scholarly reference — Buddha, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy

https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2014/entries/buddha/

The entry distinguishes the general claim that suffering arises from causes and conditions from later elaboration into a twelve-link list, and notes that shorter formulations also occur. This supports the runtime boundary already used by CSW: do not turn the twelve links into one universal fixed machine.

### Scholarly reference — Mind in Indian Buddhist Philosophy, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy

https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mind-indian-buddhism/

The entry treats dependent arising as a principle of causal/conditional dependence in Buddhist philosophy. It is useful for preserving the tradition-specific philosophical setting while keeping CSW's de-bound operations separate from claims about mind or liberation.

## Structural core under requalification

The currently adopted CSW structure can be stated without Buddhist vocabulary:

1. begin with an observed target-side state or event;
2. ask what upstream conditions are required for it to arise;
3. expand a compressed "A caused B" statement into a condition chain;
4. pair arising with cessation: if a condition is removed or changed, what downstream state should stop or change?;
5. identify conditions that are observable or modifiable;
6. test whether an apparently self-standing component actually depends on other target-side conditions;
7. return every causal or conditional claim to observations, logs, tests, experiments, or domain evidence.

The runtime question is whether this structure remains a distinct cognitive generator after comparison with strong ordinary engineering analysis.

## Current cognitive operations

### condition-chain

Replace a compressed symptom/cause statement with an upstream sequence of target-side conditions.

### upstream-condition

Move from an observed outcome toward earlier or enabling conditions rather than stopping at the nearest symptom.

### cessation-counterfactual

Ask what should cease or change if one condition is removed, repaired, or prevented.

### dependency-reframing

Re-read an apparently independent component or event as dependent on surrounding state, resources, configuration, timing, or other conditions.

### anti-essentialist-counter-view

Challenge a description that treats one component as self-standing when the target shows that its role or behavior depends on a larger system of conditions.

## Target-return questions

- What exact target state or failure is being explained?
- Which upstream conditions are observed rather than merely suggested by the framework?
- Which condition can be changed or removed?
- What target-side observation should change if that condition is actually relevant?
- Does the current chain hide branching, convergence, or multiple relation modes?
- Would Five Whys, a fault tree, or an ordinary dependency graph produce the same upstream questions?
- Does "this component depends on conditions" add any target-side distinction beyond normal systems analysis?
- If all Buddhist vocabulary is removed, which questions remain that the ordinary baseline did not already generate?

## Historical / doctrinal boundary

- Dependent origination is a Buddhist doctrine with multiple historical formulations; it is not a generic software architecture notation.
- The twelve-link formulation is important but should not be treated as the only historical form or as a universal chain template.
- The arising/cessation formula occurs in a soteriological context concerned with suffering and liberation. CSW must not erase that origin while borrowing only a structural question.
- Later Madhyamaka, Abhidhamma, Yogācāra, Tibetan, East Asian, and modern interpretations should not be collapsed into one invariant theory.
- "Conditional" in the source tradition is not permission to assert modern causal claims without target-side evidence.

## What not to import by default

- the twelve links as software or organizational categories;
- Buddhist ontology, rebirth, karma, or liberation claims;
- the idea that every target problem is best represented as one chain;
- causal certainty from mere sequence;
- a claim that removing one condition is sufficient without testing other target-side conditions;
- "everything is interconnected" as a substitute for inspectable conditions.

## De-binding

1. remove Buddhist doctrinal vocabulary unless the target is historically or philosophically about that tradition;
2. name the observed target state;
3. list only target-supported upstream conditions;
4. state the proposed intervention or removal explicitly;
5. state the expected downstream change;
6. preserve branching or uncertainty instead of forcing one chain;
7. verify the relation in the target;
8. compare the questions with ordinary RCA / fault-tree / dependency analysis before crediting the framework.

## Runtime requalification result

For the tested SIer incident, the strong ordinary baseline reproduces all current target-relevant operations.

Five Whys supplies upstream drill-down. Fault-tree analysis begins from a failure and expands necessary preconditions with AND/OR logic. Ordinary dependency analysis already treats components as non-self-standing. Counterfactual/intervention analysis directly asks what changes when one variable or condition is altered.

No additional target-side question remains that requires the dependent-origination substrate for this use.

The strongest framework-specific residue is philosophical provenance: a historically important paired arising/cessation formulation and an anti-essentialist orientation. That remains valuable for explicit Buddhist, comparative-philosophy, or historically situated work, but it does not currently justify default runtime selection complexity for general SIer analysis.

The former runtime dossier has now been removed from the default corpus. The comparison above establishes capability overlap with ordinary RCA / fault-tree / dependency analysis after the relevant cognitive operations are named.

### Discovery-value comparison

See:

The retrospective ex-ante comparison starts from ordinary post-incident practice without naming Five Whys, FTA, counterfactual causation, or dependent origination in advance.

On the tested rolling-deployment incident, ordinary reliability analysis already asks which components, conditions, actions, and events contributed; what earlier state made the failure path possible; which mitigation or corrective action prevents recurrence; and what observation should change after that intervention. The target therefore opens upstream-condition and cessation-check work before framework contact.

### Retrospective requalification result

The operational demotion is **confirmed under the discovery-aware standard**.

The reason is not that dependent origination is historically unimportant, nor that its arising / cessation formulation is conceptually identical to modern incident analysis.

The product result is narrower: for the tested general SIer incident, both the cognitive capability and the ex-ante selection path are already supplied by ordinary post-incident practice at comparable or lower selection cost. After de-binding, no distinct target-side discovery remains.

Preserve dependent origination as a sourced research candidate for explicit Buddhist, comparative-philosophy, historical, educational, or future targets where a distinct discovery contribution can be demonstrated.
