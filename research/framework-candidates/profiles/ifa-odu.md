# Ifá / Odù candidate profile

Status: profile-ready / living-tradition-sensitive / not-runtime

## Source basis

UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Ifa divination system

https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ifa-divination-system-00146

UNESCO describes 256 odù, each with a divination signature, and a much larger evolving corpus of ese verses transmitted and interpreted by trained Yoruba priests.

### Scholarly corpus reference

Karin Barber, “Ifá divination corpus, the,” Routledge Encyclopedia of Philosophy

https://www.rep.routledge.com/articles/thematic/ifa-divination-corpus-the/v-1/sections/the-corpus-of-verses

Barber describes the 256-figure system and the large poetic corpus attached to the figures, including recitation and interpretation by trained specialists. This independent scholarly source reinforces the distinction between compact signature, open oral corpus, and situated interpretation without turning the corpus into a generic retrieval template.

## Structural value for CSW

The distinctive cognitive structure is not “fortune telling.” It is a compact combinatorial signature that indexes a large poetic/narrative corpus, after which interpretation occurs in a decision context.

Potential operations:

- combinatorial-index;
- indexed-corpus-retrieval;
- poetic-analogy;
- case-by-signature;
- decision-context-reframing.

## Native operation candidates

### combinatorial-index

Treat a compact signature as an index into a larger corpus rather than as a complete interpretation.

### indexed-corpus-retrieval

Ask what kinds of narratives or cases are associated with an index, while keeping the corpus itself distinct from the index.

### case-by-signature

Compare cases reached through the same index without assuming that the index explains their outcome.

### poetic-analogy

Use a retrieved narrative to generate a question or analogy, not to establish target-side fact.

### decision-context-reframing

Ask how the relevance of a narrative changes with the decision context rather than treating one stored meaning as fixed.

## Public-source boundary

UNESCO and Routledge establish the high-level structure: 256 odù, a large and evolving corpus of ese verses, specialist recitation and interpretation, and continuing Yoruba transmission. These sources do not provide CSW with an operational corpus or authority to reproduce the practice.

Accordingly, this profile does not contain or synthesize ese, does not implement odù assignment, and does not treat public summaries as a substitute for specialist training or community context.

## Living-tradition boundary

Do not simulate babalawo authority, detach sacred vocabulary into generic productivity prompts, or treat odù assignment as target evidence. The verses carry Yoruba history, belief, language, and contemporary social context.

## Target-return questions

- What changes when a compact state points to a narrative corpus rather than a label?
- Can a retrieved story generate a question that survives after sacred/signature mapping is removed?
- What interpretive authority would be falsely implied by automation?

## De-binding

1. remove odù names, ritual assignment, divination language, and priestly authority from the target-facing result;
2. retain only the abstract distinction among compact index, larger narrative corpus, situated selection, and interpretation;
3. use only target-owned or independently authorized narratives after de-binding;
4. label any analogy as framework_generated until target evidence supports it;
5. if the useful operation requires an actual Ifá verse or ritual judgment, stop rather than inventing or simulating one.

## Adoption hold

The candidate remains research-only even though the structural value is clear. The corpus is living religious and specialist knowledge, and CSW must not substitute retrieval over public summaries for babalawo training or interpretive authority.

A safe future design would need to show that “compact signature → large narrative corpus → situated interpretation” can be used as an abstract cognitive operation without reproducing sacred assignment, verses, or priestly judgment.

## Profile-ready decision

The profile now has two independent source classes, native operations, explicit living-tradition boundaries, a de-binding route, and both positive and negative worked examples.

It remains outside runtime. The remaining blocker is not lack of structural clarity; it is that safe runtime use would need to guarantee that CSW never substitutes generated content for the living corpus or specialist interpretation.
