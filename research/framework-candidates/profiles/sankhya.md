Status: adopted / runtime-corpus

## Identity

- names: サーンキヤ / Sāṅkhya / Samkhya
- family: classical Indian philosophical traditions
- intended CSW use: production-layer and observer/process separation
- runtime dossier: src/ja-JP/frameworks/sankhya.md

## Source basis

Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Sankhya

https://iep.utm.edu/sankhya/

The reference identifies the classical system as a 25-tattva enumeration, distinguishes puruṣa from prakṛti, describes manifest/unmanifest prakṛti, the three guṇas, and warns that later accounts of the unmanifest-to-manifest relation can differ.

### Primary classical text

Īśvarakṛṣṇa, Sāṃkhyakārikā — GRETIL / SUB Göttingen

https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA-alt.htm

This digital Sanskrit text supplies a direct classical-text anchor for the kārikā layer. GRETIL records the underlying printed edition and the University of Tokyo data entry. CSW uses it to keep the adopted structural core tied to the classical text rather than reconstructing missing detail from model memory.

## Structural core

- puruṣa: witnessing/conscious person side in the classical system;
- prakṛti: productive nature;
- twenty-five tattvas in the classical formulation;
- manifest / unmanifest distinction;
- three guṇas;
- an ordered production/evolution account among prakṛti-side structures.

CSW treats these as a source-grounded cognitive field, not target ontology.

## Native operation candidates

- layer-backtracking: move from visible product to upstream generating layer.
- production-tree: externalize what is treated as producing what.
- observer-process-split: separate the standpoint describing a target from processes inside it.
- guna-mixture-probe: use three tendencies only as a temporary mixture view, never fixed typing.
- inversion: ask whether a proposed cause is itself downstream.

## Lineage / variant boundary

Do not identify the classical Sāṅkhya-Kārikā formulation with every later Hindu cosmology. The exact relation between unmanifest and manifest prakṛti has historically received differing accounts. Do not merge Sāṅkhya automatically with Yoga, Vedānta, Ayurveda, Tantra, or chakra systems.

## Exploratory outputs

- upstream layer candidate;
- observer/observed distinction;
- production relation;
- mixture question;
- alternative causal ordering.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- 25 tattvas as facts about an unrelated target;
- guṇa personality diagnosis;
- gendered metaphors as empirical psychological claims;
- automatic import of liberation, rebirth, or cosmological claims.

## De-binding

1. remove Sanskrit category labels from the target-facing candidate unless provenance matters;
2. restate the useful layer or production relation in target vocabulary;
3. keep framework_generated origin;
4. ask what observation would distinguish upstream from downstream;
5. discard the metaphysical mapping if the target relation survives without it.

## Target-return questions

- Which visible output may be a product rather than a cause?
- Is the observer's classification being confused with a process inside the target?
- Which upstream distinction must exist for this downstream state to occur?
- What remains if the three-guṇa reading is removed?

## What this framework does not license

It does not establish a 25-part target ontology, personality type, medical model, or universal cosmology.
