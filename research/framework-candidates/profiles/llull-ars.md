## Source basis

### Scholarly overview

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Ramon Llull

https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/llull/

Useful for the two major historical phases of the Ars, the late Ternary reform, the alphabet, the binary Third Figure, the rotating ternary Fourth Figure, and the role of definitions and rules.

### Scholarly monograph

Anthony Bonner, *The Art and Logic of Ramon Llull: A User's Guide*, Brill

https://brill.com/display/title/14655

Useful for keeping the Art's two main stages distinct and for avoiding the common reduction of the Ars to rotating figures alone.

### Specialist scholarly educational resource

Centre de Documentació Ramon Llull / Narpan, The principles of the Art

https://quisestlullus.narpan.net/en/principles-art

Useful for the late Art's table, binary and ternary combinations, and the fact that combinatorics served judgment and reasoning rather than enumeration for its own sake.

### Digital manuscript anchor

Science History Institute Digital Collections, Ars brevis and Ars abbreviata praedicandi, versio latinus II

https://digital.sciencehistory.org/works/txt90sk/viewer/sckkfrs

The collection provides a digitized historical manuscript witness of Ars brevis. It anchors the profile in an actual historical artifact while remaining later than Llull's own composition; CSW therefore does not treat this witness as an authorial autograph or erase textual transmission history.

## Historical phase boundary

The runtime scope is the late Ternary Art represented by the `Ars generalis ultima` and `Ars brevis`.

Do not silently mix this with the earlier Quaternary phase. The number of terms, figures, letter meanings, and relations changed substantially across revisions. In the late Art, the system was simplified around nine letters and four principal figures; the Third Figure handles binary combinations and the rotating Fourth Figure ternary combinations.

## Structural value for CSW

The Ars offers a systematic way to cross principles that spontaneous association may never pair. It is useful as a bounded combinatorial search method, not as proof by enumeration.

## Boundaries

Do not import Llull's theological conclusions as target facts. Keep Quaternary and Ternary phases historically distinct. Combination coverage is not semantic validity.
