# Confucian role / li candidate profile

Status: adopted / runtime-corpus

## Identity

- names: Confucian role / li, 儒家の役割・礼
- current scope: Analects-centered and early-Confucian material as represented in current scholarly references
- intended CSW use: relation-conditioned perspective, role/action congruence, ritual-context change, and practice-shaped disposition
- not intended use: importing historical hierarchy as a normative recommendation

## Source basis

### Primary text digital edition

Chinese Text Project, The Analects: Yan Yuan

https://ctext.org/analects/yan-yuan

Useful as a primary-text anchor for Analects 12.1, where seeing, hearing, speaking, and acting are explicitly related to li. This anchor does not by itself settle later interpretations of li or ren.


### Scholarly reference

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Confucius

https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/confucius/

Useful for source plurality, ritual psychology, social roles, reciprocal obligations, and the warning that there is no single historically uncontested "Confucius doctrine."

### Scholarly reference

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Chinese Ethics

https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-chinese/

Useful for the breadth of li from ceremony to family, friendship, village, and official conduct, and for its role in cultivating dispositions and structuring relationships.

## Structural core

The current profile retains four structures.

### Relation-conditioned action

What counts as fitting conduct depends partly on the relationship and situation.

### Role and enacted behavior

A role-name does not by itself prove that the associated conduct is being enacted. A useful CSW move is to compare claimed role, expected function, and observed behavior without treating the historical expectation as the target's norm.

### Ritual as patterned interaction

Li is not merely a list of rules. It includes patterned forms through which respect, consideration, status, and relationship are enacted.

### Practice shaping disposition

Ritual performance is described in the tradition not only as expression but as cultivation. For CSW this becomes a hypothesis generator: repeated forms may shape attention, affect, or expectation.

## Native operation candidates

### role-perspective-switch

Read the same event from the positions of different roles.

Questions:

- What becomes salient from each relation-position?
- Which obligations or expectations are symmetric, and which are not?
- Is the conflict partly a role mismatch?

### name-behavior-congruence

Compare a claimed role or title with enacted behavior.

Questions:

- What does the role-name imply in this context?
- Which behaviors support or contradict that role?
- Is the title hiding a functional mismatch?

Do not use the historical role norm as automatic target truth.

### relation-obligation-map

Externalize relationships before treating an issue as an isolated individual choice.

Questions:

- Which relation is carrying the expectation?
- Are multiple relations imposing incompatible demands?
- Which demand exists only because a relationship has been assumed?

### ritual-context-shift

Treat patterned interaction as context-changing rather than decorative.

Questions:

- What changes before and after the ritualized interaction?
- Which actions become possible, expected, or inappropriate?
- Does the same utterance function differently inside and outside the pattern?

### practice-shapes-disposition probe

Ask whether repeated conduct may be training attention or response.

Outputs:

- observation question
- behavioral contrast
- alternative mechanism
- falsifier

## What not to import by default

- historical family, gender, political, or status hierarchy as a modern norm;
- "Confucianism" as one invariant doctrine across China, Korea, Japan, Vietnam, and all periods;
- role as fixed personality or essence;
- li as generic etiquette;
- ren as generic kindness;
- claims about moral cultivation as established causal facts in the target.

## Historical and lineage caution

The Confucius corpus is historically complex. The Analects itself reflects layered transmission, and later Ru traditions developed different metaphysical and ethical systems.

This profile therefore does not automatically include:

- Mencian theories of human nature;
- Xunzian theories of ritual and names;
- Han correlative cosmology;
- Song-Ming Neo-Confucian metaphysics;
- modern "Confucian role ethics" as if it were identical to early texts.

Each may become a separate layer or candidate.

## Exploratory prompts

- Which relationship is currently defining the expected behavior?
- If the same person occupied another role, would the interpretation change?
- Is the role-name supported by actual conduct?
- Which ritual or interaction pattern is shaping the situation before any explicit decision is made?
- Are two legitimate role-demands in conflict?
- What remains of the insight after the historical norm is removed?

## De-binding

1. remove Confucian terminology unless needed for provenance;
2. restate the candidate as role, relation, expectation, interaction pattern, or repeated-practice question;
3. separate descriptive target evidence from the historical norm that generated the question;
4. preserve conflicts among roles instead of forcing harmonization;
5. do not turn relational identity into a denial of individual agency.

### Result: bounded runtime retention

For the first target, "the owner failed to decide," ordinary RACI/approval review already separates title, accountable responsibility, permission and enacted function. That is strong **capability and discovery overlap**; this use alone would not justify runtime selection.

For the second target, formal consultation is correct but an established public meeting sequence may alter whether the same "any objections?" prompt functions as invitation or closure. A generic project incident review need not select the enacted-interaction question ex ante. Role/li contact can expose that job, which organizational-routine research can subsequently investigate and express in target language. This gives **plausible bounded discovery value**, not unique analytic capability and not an experimentally demonstrated causal effect.

Retain the runtime dossier for patterned relational interaction, role-conditioned meaning, and repeated practice **only when these are missing jobs**. Do not activate merely because a role title, stakeholder, RACI chart, or disagreement exists. If a generic facilitation/organizational-routines review has already considered enactment and independent participant interpretations, no-framework is valid.

The bounded target return is observational: meeting sequence, formal authority, participant accounts, alternative hypotheses, and falsifiers. Do not import historical hierarchy, prescribe subordination, infer silent participants' mental states, or claim that repeated ritual caused a disposition without target-side evidence.
