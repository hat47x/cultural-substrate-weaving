## Decision

### Five aggregates

The candidate is bounded to experience decomposition and anti-reification.

Its value is not a five-part taxonomy. It separates bodily/material condition, feeling, perception/interpretation, formations or action tendencies, and awareness inside an account of sentient experience.

### Jo-Ha-Kyū

The candidate is bounded to Zeami/Noh-centered temporal articulation.

Its value is not beginning/middle/end. It asks about temporal function, tempo transition, acceleration/deceleration, and closure shape.

It remains outside runtime until these operations survive across structurally different non-artistic targets without becoming a universal three-stage recipe.

This broadens the encounter function without increasing the adopted runtime before the candidate corpus is sufficiently mature.
