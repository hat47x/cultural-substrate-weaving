# Framework corpus ninth wave — 2026-10-02

Status: candidate-quality expansion / no runtime adoption

## Decision

Ifá / Odù advances from research-only to profile-ready, but remains explicitly outside runtime.

The structural contribution is strong and independently sourced: a compact combinatorial signature indexes a large and evolving narrative corpus, after which trained specialists select, recite, and interpret material in a decision context.

The runtime blocker is therefore not structural ambiguity. It is authority and corpus handling.

CSW must not:

- assign odù as a divination act;
- invent, paraphrase as authentic, or synthesize ese;
- simulate babalawo authority;
- use public summaries as if they were the living corpus.

The safe abstraction is narrower: compact index → larger target-owned corpus → situated selection → interpretation. Positive and negative worked examples now make that boundary executable.

## Sefirot research split

The two lineage-specific tracks created in the previous wave advance from research-only to sourced-candidate:

- Sefer Yetzirah dimensions;
- later theosophical sefirot.

The parent `sefirot` entry remains a research umbrella. Neither child is profile-ready until its historical scope and de-binding operations are independently specified.

## Product-value effect

The candidate pool gains a high-quality indexed-narrative architecture without weakening cultural fidelity. This is preferable to adopting a superficial “story lookup” module that would erase the living tradition that made the structure meaningful.
