# Huayan vs Confucian role / li

Status: runtime requalification / near-neighbor differentiation

## Shared surface

Both frameworks can make context and role visible. They should not therefore be collapsed into one "relationships matter" family.

## Huayan

Primary job: part/whole co-definition.

Questions:

- What is this part because it belongs to this whole?
- If the whole changes, which identity changes?
- What becomes visible from another node?
- Which difference must survive integration?

## Confucian role / li

Primary job: enacted relational position and patterned conduct.

Questions:

- What role is claimed?
- What behavior enacts it?
- What obligations arise in the relation?
- What repeated practices shape expectations?

## Same organizational target

For a Platform Security team called a "shared service":

- Huayan asks whether Platform Security has the same identity/function in each product whole and whether centralization erases product-specific roles.
- Confucian role/li asks whether the team actually enacts the obligations implied by "shared service" and how product/security roles are performed.

## Selection boundary

Use Huayan for whole/part identity, context re-identification, node perspective, and integration-with-difference.

Use Confucian role/li for actor relation-position, claimed vs enacted role, obligation, and patterned conduct.

Framework-level distinctness prevents selection collapse; Huayan's actual retention evidence comes from the separate discovery-value comparison.
