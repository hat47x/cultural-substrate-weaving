# Inka khipu / generic tree / provenance boundary

Status: profile-readiness boundary

## Inka khipu record structure

Primary question: what information is distributed across value, attachment, position, grouping, and other channels, and what is lost by flattening?

A central discipline is preserving unknown-but-structured features without inventing semantics.

## Generic tree

Primary question: what are the parent/child relations?

A tree may be sufficient when attachment hierarchy is the only meaningful structure. Khipu adds value only if multiple channels or flattening loss matter.

## Hadith isnād / matn

Primary question: what is the transmitted content and what is its transmission path?

This is provenance/transmission separation, not multi-channel encoding inside one artifact.

## Runtime boundary

Do not choose khipu because a target has hierarchy alone. Choose it only when the missing cognitive operation is preservation of multiple representational channels or explicit handling of structured-but-uninterpreted features.
