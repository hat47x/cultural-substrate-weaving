# Five aggregates / dependent origination / Taiheki boundary

Status: profile-readiness boundary

## Five aggregates

Primary question: what different processes are being collapsed into a unitary account of sentient experience or self-description?

## Dependent origination

Primary question: what conditions support arising or cessation?

A distinction among feeling, perception, and formations does not by itself establish a causal chain.

## Taiheki

Primary question: what embodied response pattern or observation axis becomes visible through bodily tension, release, posture, or response?

Five aggregates must not become a body type or stable person classification.

## Research rule

Select five-aggregate decomposition only when the target contains an experience/self-description that benefits from process separation. Reject it when the target is merely a five-part structure, a non-sentient technical object, or a problem whose missing operation is causal rather than experiential.
