# Jo-Ha-Kyū / rites of passage / periodic systems boundary

Status: profile-readiness boundary

## Jo-Ha-Kyū

Primary question: how does pace, transition shape, or closure intensity change within a process?

## Rites of passage

Primary question: has the target separated from an old status, entered an intermediate state, and become incorporated into a new status?

This is a state-transition schema, not a tempo profile.

## Maya calendars and other periodic systems

Primary question: what recurs, at what phase relation, and over what time scale?

Recurrence does not imply jo-ha-kyū pacing.

## Research rule

Use jo-ha-kyū only when target-side timing or event structure can support a pacing distinction. Reject it when it merely renames first/middle/last thirds or when pace is constant.
