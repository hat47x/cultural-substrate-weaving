# Llull Ars / Yijing / Catuṣkoṭi operation boundary

Status: runtime adoption boundary

## Llull's late Ars

Primary question: which explicitly chosen distinctions have not yet been crossed?

Strong use: systematic binary/ternary crossing, unseen-combination probes, and expansion of question space.

## Yijing

Primary question: how does a change in a position alter a canonical configuration or state?

Strong use: position, combination, local change, neighboring or transformed configurations.

## Catuṣkoṭi

Primary question: is a proposition trapped in a binary frame?

Strong use: P, not-P, both, neither, and predicate audit.

## Runtime rule

Do not substitute one for another because all three can generate alternatives.

- Use Llull when the missing operation is systematic crossing among several distinctions.
- Use Yijing when the missing operation is positional configuration and change.
- Use Catuṣkoṭi when the missing operation is reopening the logical frame of one proposition.

For Llull, target-constraint pruning is mandatory after generation and remains a CSW-added operation, not part of the historical framework.
