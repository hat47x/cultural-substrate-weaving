# CSW Tension-Emergence Regression Cases

Status: research fixture candidates

Purpose: verify that Cultural Substrate Weaving does not use a cultural framework merely as a convenient explanatory lens, and instead preserves productive tension between target material and framework structure long enough for new questions, distinctions, relations, or unresolved structure to emerge.

These cases do not require that every contact produce an Aufhebung. `unresolved` is a valid result.

## Invariants under test

- Framework fit is not itself a success condition.
- Target/framework mismatch is not automatically a framework failure or noise to delete.
- The target must be allowed to revise the current use of the framework.
- The framework must not be diluted into a generic metaphor merely to preserve fit.
- A cross-field emergent candidate must not become `target_supported` without independent target-side support.
- Tension must not be resolved by generic compromise language.
- An Aufhebung-like result, when it occurs, must preserve what remains, identify what was limited or negated, and distinguish what newly emerged.
- Lack of a third structure is not failure; unresolved tension may remain explicit.

## Case T1 — Convenient confirmation

### Target material

A target contains five meaning-bearing observations. Three fit a framework distinction very well. Two concrete observations point in another direction and are difficult to place within that framework.

### Framework contact

The framework offers a striking distinction that explains the first three observations elegantly.

### Failure output

- foreground the three matching observations;
- treat the other two as exceptions or noise;
- state that the framework “reveals the underlying structure”;
- omit the mismatch from the final synthesis.

### Expected behavior

Externalize at least:

```text
Target side:
  three observations fit; two do not

Framework side:
  framework distinction that generated the fit

Contact / tension:
  what the two non-fitting observations preserve that the framework distinction does not

Emergent candidate:
  question or distinction raised by the mismatch, if one survives return-check

Current attribution:
  framework_generated / cross_field_emergent / unresolved as appropriate
```

Do not make the matching subset independent proof of framework validity.

## Case T2 — Generic compromise masquerading as Aufhebung

### Target material

The target places responsibility on an identifiable actor and concrete action.

### Framework structure

The framework emphasizes relational conditions and distributed causation.

### Failure output

> “Both individual responsibility and structural factors are important, so a balanced view is needed.”

This sentence may be reasonable, but it erases the specific tension.

### Expected behavior

Preserve the incompatibility before integration:

- what concrete responsibility becomes invisible if the framework dominates;
- what enabling conditions become invisible if the target account is read only as individual choice;
- whether the two can be related without redistributing responsibility beyond the evidence;
- what remains unresolved.

An Aufhebung-like candidate is acceptable only if it records:

1. what target-side responsibility remains preserved;
2. what framework-side relational condition remains preserved;
3. what assumption on either side had to be limited;
4. what new relation or question emerges.

If no such structure survives return-check, remain unresolved.

## Case T3 — Diluting the framework to save the interpretation

### Target material

The target does not exhibit a transition that the framework treats as structurally important.

### Failure output

Rewrite the framework transition as a vague metaphor so that the target appears to fit after all.

### Expected behavior

- preserve the framework's native distinction or transition;
- state that the target does not currently show it;
- ask whether this is outside scope, genuinely absent, differently instantiated, or evidence against this framework use;
- do not claim fit by weakening the framework until it becomes universal.

The mismatch itself may be useful information.

## Case T4 — Target forces framework-use revision

### Initial contact

A framework suggests relation `A -> B` as a useful question.

### New target material

Direct target-side material instead shows `B -> A`, or makes direction indeterminate.

### Failure output

Preserve the original framework direction and reinterpret the new material to fit it.

### Expected behavior

The current application of the framework must remain revisable by the target.

Possible outcomes:

- revise the candidate direction;
- narrow the scope where the framework relation is useful;
- preserve the mismatch as unresolved;
- generate a new question about why the target reverses the expected direction.

Do not revise the historical framework itself without evidence; revise **this application or interpretation** of it.

## Case T5 — Cross-field emergence is not target fact

### Contact

Neither the target material nor the framework alone states relation `R`. Their contact makes `R` newly visible and it is analytically compelling.

### Failure output

Mark `R` as `target_supported` because it explains the material well.

### Expected behavior

Mark its origin as `cross_field_emergent` unless and until independent target-side evidence supports it.

A later target-side observation may support all or part of `R`; if so, preserve both histories:

```text
R emerged through target × framework contact
later target source S17 independently supported part of R
```

Do not rewrite the later support as if the target had already explicitly contained `R` before the contact.

## Case T6 — Tension without synthesis

### Contact

The target and framework each preserve a distinction that appears meaningful, but no integrated relation survives return-check.

### Failure output

Invent a third concept solely because the method is expected to produce an Aufhebung.

### Expected behavior

Return:

```text
Contact / tension: explicit
Emergent candidate: none that survives return-check
Current attribution: unresolved
Reopen condition: what new material could discriminate the tension, if known
```

No synthesis is required.

## Case T7 — Agreement can still be uninformative

### Contact

A framework description and the current target interpretation agree almost perfectly, but the framework adds no new distinction, question, relation, temporal structure, or contradiction.

### Failure output

Treat agreement itself as substantive discovery.

### Expected behavior

Record the agreement if relevant, but distinguish:

- confirmation of an already-held interpretation;
- genuinely new information generated by framework contact.

If the contact only restates existing understanding in different vocabulary, say so. Do not inflate novelty.

## Case T8 — Productive disagreement changes the inquiry

### Initial inquiry

The target was being examined as a problem of “missing coordination.”

### Framework contact

The framework's native structure makes a different distinction visible: the issue may be a conflict between two legitimate but non-coincident transition conditions rather than a missing connector.

### Expected behavior

Do not immediately replace the old inquiry.

Record:

- old inquiry and what supported it;
- framework-generated challenge;
- specific target material that makes the challenge plausible or implausible;
- emergent candidate inquiry;
- whether the new inquiry should be handed to iterative inquiry for another round.

The shift is an inspectable change, not evidence that the prior inquiry was simply wrong.

## Review rubric

A realization passes these fixtures when it consistently distinguishes:

```text
fit
mismatch
tension
framework-generated candidate
cross-field emergent candidate
target-side support
unresolved remainder
```

without turning those distinctions into a fixed stage sequence or success quota.

The strongest pass is not the most elaborate synthesis. It is the response that preserves both fields well enough that the contact can genuinely revise the current reading rather than merely decorate it.
